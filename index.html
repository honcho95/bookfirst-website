Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Bookside ("we", "our", "the app") is built privacy-first. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives, and your rights. Short version: your reading life stays on your device unless you choose to share it with friends; we never sell data and we show no ads.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Your book library (titles, authors, covers, progress)
  • Your reading sessions, notes, quotes, saved words and statistics
  • Your reading goal, schedule and preferences

None of this is sent to our servers unless you create an optional social account and choose to share it (see section 3).

2. App blocking & Screen Time

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time (FamilyControls) framework. The apps you select are represented by opaque tokens that never leave your device — we cannot see which apps you choose or how you use them. You can revoke Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Optional social account

The friends features require signing in with Apple. If you do, the following is stored on our servers (hosted on Supabase, in the United States):

  • Your email address — or Apple's private relay address, if you chose to hide it. It identifies your account and nothing else: we never send marketing email
  • Your handle and display name
  • The reading data you share: books (including cover images), finished sessions, session notes, reviews and quotes — except anything you mark as private, which never syncs
  • Your friends list, likes, comments and reports
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications (used only for friend activity — never marketing; you can turn social notifications off in Settings)

Friends can only see your shared data once you have accepted each other. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account — this removes your profile and everything you shared from our servers. Your local library stays on your device.

4. Analytics (opt-out)

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage events (screens viewed, buttons tapped) to improve the app. No names, no book data, no notes, no identifiers for advertising, no cross-app tracking, no IDFA. You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings → Privacy. PostHog Privacy Policy

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which receives anonymized transaction data to unlock your membership across devices. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

6. Third-party lookups

  • Book search — your search query or scanned ISBN is sent to Google Books and Open Library to find book details. No personal data is attached.
  • Word definitions — the word you look up is sent to dictionary services (Wiktionary via FreeDictionaryAPI). Nothing else is attached.

7. What we never do

  • No location, contacts or message access
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No ads, no selling or renting of data — to anyone, ever

8. Your rights

You can export or delete your local data by managing your library in the app, delete your social account in-app (section 3), and contact us for any privacy request at support@getbookfirst.com. If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the rights provided by the GDPR, including access, rectification and erasure.

9. Children

Bookside is not directed at children under 13, and the social features require an account under Apple's minimum age for Sign in with Apple in your region.

10. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
